Just Sit With Me

When Understanding Is Misunderstood

The book starts with a deceptively simple question: what do we actually mean when we say we understand another person?

It examines what happens when advice arrives before reception, when familiar categories shrink someone’s experience, and when a person knows what they mean but cannot always access the words in real time.

The companion workbook is designed to help readers bring those ideas into their own relationships, communication patterns, and lived experience.
